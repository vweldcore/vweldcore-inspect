Quick Reference

Technical notes from VWELDCORE engineering. Works offline.
Temperature & insulation
Room typeTempPU panel

Panel core: high-density polyurethane, 40–42 kg/m³. Baseline design assumes Nigerian ambient of +35 to +40 °C.

Power supply
  • Small walk-in chillers: single-phase (220–240 V) or 3-phase.
  • Blast freezers, 800 L chillers and 100–200 block ice machines: 3-phase 380–415 V, 50 Hz, from utility or diesel generator.
Stainless steel: 304 vs 316L
  • AISI 304: food processing, commercial kitchens, dairy and cold room shelving.
  • AISI 316L: adds molybdenum for high-salinity, acidic, chemical or pharmaceutical environments.
  • Finishes: 2B or sanitary polish. Welds must be crevice-free TIG/MIG.

How we execute a project
  1. Inquiry & thermal scoping: engineering response within 24 hours.
  2. Site assessment & BOQ: site access, power (3-phase / generator) and hygiene requirements checked.
  3. Workshop fabrication: TIG/MIG stainless work and PU panel assembly in Lagos.
  4. Installation & commissioning: erection, vacuum/pressure tests, pull-down verification, operator training.
